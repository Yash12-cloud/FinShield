SANGYAN is a 4-day online hackathon organized by SNTC, IIT (BHU) Varanasi, in collaboration with SEBI and NSDL.

The hackathon challenges participants to build technology-driven solutions that make investing safer, more accessible, and easier to understand for India's growing retail investor community.

India has seen rapid growth in retail participation, particularly among first-time investors and users from Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities. At the same time, investors increasingly encounter financial scams, misleading online content, complex disclosures, and unfamiliar grievance processes.

SANGYAN focuses on building practical solutions that help investors detect fraud, understand financial information, develop safer financial habits, and become more resilient to misinformation and scams.

Why Participate?
Build technology that tackles real-world financial fraud, scams, and misinformation.

Solve meaningful problems faced by first-time and retail investors across India.

Explore AI, ML, NLP, voice interfaces, content analysis, and other emerging technologies.

Design inclusive solutions for Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities, regional-language users, elderly users, and people with different levels of digital literacy.

Work on problems directly connected to investor protection and financial resilience.

Build a working prototype around a genuine investor problem.

Get an opportunity to present your solution to a jury involving stakeholders from the financial ecosystem.

Develop solutions that have the potential to evolve beyond a hackathon prototype into real-world public-good products.

Evaluation Criteria
1. Investor Resilience & Safety
How effectively does the solution help users avoid fraud, reduce potential harm, or develop safer financial behaviour?

2. Bharat-First Usability & Accessibility
Solutions should consider regional languages, low-bandwidth environments, accessibility, voice interfaces, and users with varying levels of digital and financial literacy.

3. Trust, Privacy & Guardrail Compliance
Solutions should follow privacy-by-design principles, communicate uncertainty clearly, and comply with the hackathon's investor protection guidelines.

4. Technical Execution & Real-World Feasibility
Technology should be used meaningfully to solve the problem. Teams should demonstrate a functional prototype and a realistic path towards deployment.

5. Impact & Scalability
Solutions should demonstrate meaningful potential to benefit investors and scale across different regions, languages, and user groups in India.

Important Guardrails
SANGYAN is focused on investor protection and public-good technology, rather than investment promotion or trading.

Submissions must not:

Provide stock tips or buy, sell, or hold recommendations.

Predict stock prices or investment outcomes.

Build systems intended to drive speculative investment decisions.

Promote specific brokers, financial products, or investment schemes.

Use monetisation mechanisms such as broking commissions or investment upsells.

Collect OTPs, SMS data, or sensitive financial information without appropriate authorization.

Teams are encouraged to follow a privacy-first and safety-first approach throughout their solution.

Who Should You Build For?
SANGYAN focuses on real problems faced by:

First-time investors from Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities.

Regional-language users who may find English-heavy financial interfaces difficult to navigate.

Young investors exposed to financial advice and investment tips through social media and messaging platforms.

Families dealing with fake investment schemes, phishing attempts, and financial fraud.

Elderly investors managing investments, nominees, and financial assets.

Users with limited digital or financial literacy.

Hackathon Format
Team Size: 1 to 4 members

Mode: Online

Duration: 4 days

Participation: Open to college students across India

Registration Fee: Free

Certificate: All participants receive a certificate

Participants can use open-source technologies and commercially available APIs, subject to the hackathon guidelines and appropriate disclosure of third-party components.

What to Build?
Teams are encouraged to identify a specific investor problem and develop a focused, working solution around it.

Your submission should demonstrate:

A clearly defined problem and target user.

A functional prototype addressing the identified problem.

A clear explanation of how the solution improves investor resilience.

The technical architecture and technologies used.

A realistic user journey or demonstration.

The potential impact and scalability of the solution.

Submission Requirements
Teams should submit:

Working Prototype: Demonstrate the core functionality of your solution.

Problem Statement: Clearly explain the investor problem being addressed.

Solution Overview: Explain how your solution works and why it is useful.

Technology Details: Describe the architecture, AI/ML components, APIs, datasets, and other technologies used.

Demo Video: Provide a 3 to 5 minute demonstration of the working prototype.

Impact & Scalability: Explain the expected impact and how the solution could scale across India.
